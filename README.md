# Women Safety Crime Analytics Dashboard — India 2024

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing crime against women 
across all Indian states and UTs using official NCRB (National 
Crime Records Bureau) data.

## Dashboard Visuals
- **Map** — Crime rate by state (bubble size = severity)
- **Bar Chart** — Top 10 states by crime rate (2024)
- **Line Chart** — Crime trend across states (2022–2024)
- **Scatter Plot** — Crime rate vs chargesheeting rate 
  (identifies states with high crime + low justice delivery)

## Key Insights
- Delhi (131) and Telangana (129) have the highest crime rates
- Several high-crime states show low chargesheeting rates, 
  indicating justice delivery gaps
- Overall national crime count shows marginal decline from 
  2022 to 2024

## Dataset
- Source: NCRB (National Crime Records Bureau), Government of India
- Table 3A.1 — Crime Against Women (IPC+SLL) 2022–2024
- Covers 28 states and 8 Union Territories
